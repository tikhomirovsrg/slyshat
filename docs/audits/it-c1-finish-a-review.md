## Continuity and evidence

- All branches retain their opening facts. The illustrator has voluntarily
  signed the next contract in both branches; the present contract allows private
  drawing. The baker's trial is paid and its volume can be reduced in either
  branch. The brooch has already been found before the seamstress chooses.
- The travel competition is explicitly free and its actual prize is a suitcase
  in both routes. The choice changes when the friends read the conditions and
  how they discuss ownership, not whether their code wins.
- Both cinema routes lead to checking the same incorrect thermostat schedule.
  The difference is handling the request and the surrounding conversation.
- The button is a wooden spare, not a missing functional fastening. No character
  moves heavy furniture without a trained technician. The photo-route ends with
  the client informed where the spare has landed.
- Opening and branch questions test intention or implication in the exact
  referenced turn. Phrase anchors are exact substrings. Distractors were
  revised to represent plausible but unsupported interpretations instead of
  unrelated jokes. Correctness and evidence stay within the visited route.
